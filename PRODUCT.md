# PRODUCT — NJ Scatman pitch page

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (no frameworks, no tracking)
- **Built from:** Muse brief 2026-10-08, plus nj-scatman.com fetched 2026-10-08

## What it is
A focused "First week FREE — pick your plan" landing page for NJ Scatman, a locally owned
pet waste removal (pooper-scooper) service in South Jersey. Linkable from Facebook posts and
from their existing site. Not a replacement for every page of nj-scatman.com.

## Users
1. **The owner** (name unknown) — sees the pitch first, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
2. **Dog owners in Burlington, Camden and Gloucester counties** — scrolling Facebook groups like
   "Marlton NJ Local Business Group", deciding whether to try a free week.

## Purpose
Turn "I'd like my yard scooped" into a request in under a minute: pick a plan and dog count,
see the price, give a town, and send it by call, text or email. Today every signup has to
call or email with no guidance (brief, "Why they're a prospect").

## Positioning
Local, reliable, a bit cheeky. Against franchises (brief names POOP 911): locally owned and
operated, first week free, cancel anytime.

## Capabilities and constraints
- Contact: phone 609-582-3709 (texts **unconfirmed**), email Njscatman@gmail.com. No booking system.
- The plan picker composes a message; it does not book anything or take payment.
- Must work inside Facebook's in-app browser: `tel:`, `sms:`, `mailto:` only; no popups.

## Brand commitments (from their own materials)
- Logo: Doberman in a fedora and suit inside a green oval, brown rounded wordmark "NJ Scatman" (nj-scatman.com).
- Mascot art: same Doberman crouching beside a yellow "First Week is FREE" starburst (nj-scatman.com, 2026/10 upload).
- Voice: punny. "I DOO the dirty work!", "Scoop Dog Poo!" (Facebook post + flyer, 2026-10-08).
- Colors (inferred from logo): lawn green, chocolate brown, mustard yellow.

  cancel anytime; deodorize/sanitize and fly bag add-ons; waste haul-away; locally owned and operated.
- Flyer: "Kennel grade disinfectant used on all tools after every cleanup".

- Prices (nj-scatman.com, 2026-10-08, "Prices do not include tax"): weekly 1 dog $19 / 2 $21.50 /
  3 $24 / 4 $26.50 per cleanup; bi-weekly 1 dog $32.50 / 2 $37 per cleanup; one-time scoop
  $40 per 30 minutes. Twice-a-week list on the site looks garbled (only "3 Dogs – $19",
  "4 Dogs – $2o") — not used. *The brief said no prices were published; the site does publish them.*
- Area: brief says Burlington, Camden, Gloucester; the site's map also shades Atlantic (unconfirmed).
- Photos: their own dog photos (a white-and-tan terrier, "ZEUS" tag) from nj-scatman.com.

## Principles
1. Phone first: offer + primary action in the first screen.
2. Only their facts; prices shown exactly as published, with "plus tax".
3. Keep the pun voice, never gross. Cartoon, not graphic.
4. Leave out the shelter-dog content (brief instruction).
